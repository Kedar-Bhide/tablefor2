# Table For 2 🍽️

A low-friction meal tracker designed to work in two ways: **Solo mode** for personal tracking, or **Partner mode** to share the journey. You log your food, and the AI handles the data entry.

## Features

- **Photo-first Logging**: Snap a photo and move on. AI analyzes ingredients, portion size, and macros instantly.
- **Modes**: Use it solo or sync with a partner to see a shared feed of meals.
- **Smart Goals**: Set your own targets or let the AI calculate nutrient goals based on your profile and weight goals.
- **Monthly Recaps**: A deep dive into your monthly patterns with AI-driven behavioral coaching.
- **Bi-monthly Weight Check-ins**: Bi-monthly insights that connect your actual eating habits to weight trends.
- **Streaks & Badges**: Simple tracking for consistency and meal frequency.
